Privacy policy

The reefmap-upload app is used only by its owner to upload data files to the owner's own Google Drive.

It uses the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, so it can only see and change files it has created itself. It does not read other files, and it does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com, under Security, Third-party apps and services.
